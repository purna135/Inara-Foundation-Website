INARA NGP
Inara was a long due vision to start in nagpur. And finally it started on 1st of July through a WhatsApp group with just 2 people on board. Fast forward to now we are a team of 83+ volunteers within just 5 months. Inara’s nagpur chapter is trying its best to follow the footprints of what goes around in Bhuvneshwar with a few different twists of its own. 
We are trying to build a great community here in Nagpur for people to explore their potential while serving society in meaningful ways. Every project has a different person as it leads to enhancing leadership skills in every volunteer. Within just 5 months there are volunteers who feel the essence of Inara and treat it as their own. 

PROJECTS AT INARA NAGPUR

1.
THE OLD AGE HOME VISIT
We visited the old age home before Diwali and it was so wholesome. The volunteers felt so touched and fulfilled meeting all the grandparents there. This project was a great milestone for us all since it was a difficult project to pull off considering the Health issues and limitation the people there had. We had to be extremely cautious of the words we used and their actions. It was difficult to make conversations with them but every volunteer gave their best in them to make it the best time for the grandparents. In all sense it was one of the best projects so far. 

2.  SURAKSHA: the safe touch project 

This project was about spreading awareness about good touch and bad touch. We conducted this in a government school in Nagpur with 5th grade students and it truly left a great impact on them. The kids were very active throughout the whole orientation. We included a skit, a story time followed by a worksheet and a few games related to the topic. The motto was to make it very clear in their minds and to make sure they protect themselves. The topic is important for both the boys and the girls and that was very clearly established. The volunteers worked very hard to decide the flow of the event and it was very well executed.

3. Acointier 
Since Inara was new in Nagpur, we conducted this one to make volunteers interact and feel comfortable around each other. We had such a blast. We did this around friendship day to bring in nostalgic moments of how friendship bands were exchanged during schools and tuitions. We played multiple rounds of charades to top the serious convos that took place before it. We conducted this at a very cozy cafe which was also newly opened to help it reach a wide audience.
