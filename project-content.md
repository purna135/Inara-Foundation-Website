Project Wise Content :

RAJA CELEBRATION AT ADRUTA FONDLING HOME :  On 13 June 2025, volunteers of Inara Foundation celebrated Raja with 30 girls at Adruta Fondling Home, fostering joy and inclusion. This festival of womanhood offered underprivileged children a chance to feel recognized and valued. We distributed Raja kits with bangles, bindis, combs, clips, and ribbons. Children also received Poda Pitha, fruits, traditional sweets and handwritten notes with warm wishes. Our volunteers ensured every child felt included and valued. The day was filled with laughter and excitement as children enjoyed the cultural celebration. Through such initiatives, we aim to spread happiness, promote inclusivity and empower young girls so that no child is left out during festive occasions.


AHH VIST : Inara volunteers strive to bring laughter, joy, and creativity to patients during their challenging treatment journeys. On August 30th, the team visited Acharya Harihar Hospital, arriving full of creative ideas and enthusiasm. At Inara, we believe that emotional well-being is as important as physical care, and our activities reflect this philosophy. 

Children were given drawings to color, immersing themselves in the activity and creating vibrant, beautiful artwork. Meanwhile, adults found relaxation and focus in Mandala coloring, enjoying a moment of calm amidst their treatment routines. During the festive pre Diwali season, volunteers returned with another engaging activity. 

Adults were provided with diyas and decoration materials, while children worked alongside volunteers to create festive crafts. The heartfelt response from participants was a joy to witness, and the sessions offered a memorable and uplifting experience for everyone involved.


RAKSHA BANDHAN WORKSHOP AT INARA : On July 27, participants and volunteers at Inara enjoyed a fun-filled Raksha Bandhan Workshop, creating beautiful handcrafted Raakhis.

 The sessions were held in two slots : morning and evening, and welcomed many first-time Raakhi makers, who surprised themselves with their creativity. The Sunday was alive with craft-making, letter-writing, and celebration of the cherished occasion. Participants proudly displayed their Raakhis, sharing their creative processes and exchanging tips. Many were delighted by their own artistic abilities, at the vibrant colors and intricate designs they had crafted. 

The workshop encouraged a strong sense of community with enthusiasm and appreciation flowing freely among all participants.


PROJECT AMRIT : From June 3 to 5, during the peak of summer heat, Inara collaborated with People for Animals to bring relief to both people and animals through Project Amrit. Water containers were set up across the city to help birds and stray animals stay hydrated in the scorching weather. 

Throughout the three days, Inara volunteers distributed refreshments to traffic officers and security guards enduring long hours in the sun. On the second day, they also shared a healthy, homemade summer drink with auto drivers, bus drivers, and commuters, a simple gesture that carried immense warmth and gratitude. 

Project Amrit embodied the core value of Inara: small acts of service can create a ripple effect, building community resilience and spreading hope amid global challenges. 

Over 500 people benefited from this initiative, making this summer not just a season of heat, but of humanity, compassion, and collective joy.


IIIT BHUBANESWAR CANCER SHELTER VISIT : On January 18, 2025, Inara Foundation volunteers, along with students from IIIT Bhubaneswar, spent a beautiful day at the CanKids Cancer Shelter near SUM Hospital, Bhubaneswar, as part of the Vishist 2025 initiative. Volunteers from both Inara and IIIT Bhubaneswar came together to engage with the children, bringing smiles and moments of joy that brightened the day. 

The visit was filled with excitement, stories, and gentle moments of connection that touched many hearts. From creative interactions to simply being present, the day was a reminder that even the smallest acts of care can mean so much. 

This collaborative effort reflects a shared belief that everyone deserves the chance to give, to care, and to make a difference. Inara Foundation continues to cherish such moments, where compassion feels real and hope feels closer.


HOLI WITH SAHAYA KIDS : On March 12, Inara Foundation celebrated the festival of colours with the children of Sahaya School of Special Education, Cuttack. The day was filled with laughter, music, and joy,  making it a truly memorable celebration for everyone involved.

Our volunteers distributed eco-friendly colours, sweet packs, and fun stationery items, while the children joined in for singing, dancing, music jams, and playful activities. 

Every moment was a celebration of inclusion : a reminder that festivals are for everyone, and no one should ever feel left out or different when celebrating. It was more than just a festival; it was a day of nostalgia, connection, and shared happiness with our extended Sahaya family. 

The smiles, laughter, and memories created that day will be cherished by all. Through events like this, Inara Foundation continues to spread the colours of happiness, proving that even the smallest acts of care can make a big difference.

DAY OF SERVICE : On March 30, 2025, as part of the Bakul Day of Service, Inara Foundation volunteers engaged with children across Hatiasuni and Ishaneswar slums, contributing to a larger initiative that reached over 3,000 children across 20 slum areas. Through storytelling, arts and crafts, and games, our volunteers created a joyful and engaging environment that fostered creativity, learning, and connection. The initiative continued on September 21, when our volunteers once again joined hands for the Day of Service, visiting the Ishaneswar and Dhirukuti slums. 

They brought the same energy, warmth, and dedication to connect with the children and make the day truly memorable. From making colorful crafts to sharing stories and listening to theirs, volunteers and children bonded beautifully, turning simple moments into lasting memories.

At the heart of this initiative is the Bakul Foundation, a driving force for meaningful community service. Their commitment to creating spaces of learning and happiness made this collaboration possible, and we are deeply grateful for the trust they have placed in the Inara Foundation. 

We extend our heartfelt appreciation to the volunteers of both Bakul and Inara. It is through such compassionate hands and hearts that real change begins. True to its name, the Day of Service became a day of giving of time, effort and love, bringing smiles to young faces and nurturing bright, curious minds.


CANCER SHELTER VISITS : At the Inara Foundation, our regular visits to the CanKids Cancer Shelter are a heartfelt ritual of care and resilience. Over the months of March and April, we began our engagement with interactive science activity sessions, turning treatment days into moments of curiosity and laughter. Our volunteers conducted fun, hands-on experiments and shared fresh fruits and homemade treats, bringing joy while nurturing both body and mind.

 On May 15, we introduced storytelling sessions to offer emotional comfort and spark imagination. A collaborative effort with Sujit Mahapatra Sir from the Bakul Foundation filled the room with warmth and wonder, rekindling the inner child in everyone present. During June and July, we hosted a Ramp Walk to celebrate the children’s incredible journeys.

Cheers and applause from our volunteers made every child feel seen and valued, and the day ended on a sweet note with homemade waffles lovingly prepared by one of our volunteers. We continued the celebration with creative collage-making, games, crafts, and dancing, filling every corner with laughter and joy. The children made bracelets and other crafts, adding their own spark of creativity to each piece. 

Our visits culminated in a Rakhi-Making Workshop on July 24, where children and parents came together to craft beautiful rakhis with love. These were later sent to the brave hearts of the Indian Army, spreading the children’s happiness far beyond the shelter. This ongoing journey of connection, creativity, and care continues to reaffirm our belief that small acts of kindness can make a world of difference.
